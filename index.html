How kHYPE rewards work

kHYPE does not rebase: your balance stays the same and the exchange rate grows as validator rewards land, so each kHYPE redeems for more HYPE over time. There is nothing to claim and no fixed APY, the rate in the header is read live from Kinetiq's StakingAccountant contract on HyperEVM.

Kinetiq takes 10% of staking rewards, per its docs, 70% of that fee buys KNTQ and 30% goes to its treasury. Vector Pad adds no fee and never custodies the position, stake and unstake calls go straight from your wallet to Kinetiq's StakingManager.

What is Vector Pad

Vector Pad is a front-end for open protocols on Elysium. Elysium is an Arbitrum Orbit layer-2 built by Kinetiq that settles to HyperEVM and uses HYPE as its native gas token (chain ID 99901). Vector Pad gives you one interface for four things: launching a token, swapping, staking HYPE, and bridging between HyperEVM and Elysium. Underneath, it talks to protocols that already live on-chain, Uniswap V2 for pools and swaps, the Vector Pad launchpad contracts for launches, and Kinetiq's official bridge for moving HYPE.

It is not a custodian. Vector Pad holds no funds, runs no accounts, and has no admin keys over your assets. There is nothing to deposit and nothing to withdraw from Vector Pad itself, because your assets never leave your wallet or the protocol you are using.

Migration status: Vector Pad previously ran on Solana. The app has been rebuilt for Elysium: wallet connect, balances, the board's chain stats and Uniswap V2 + V4 swaps are live on Elysium mainnet now. The launchpad contracts (bonding curve, staking) are prepared and tested but not yet deployed, the pages that depend on them say so plainly and send nothing until the deployment lands.

How every action works

Every transaction follows the same pattern:

  1. Build: the app prepares the transaction in your browser.
  2. Simulate: it is simulated against the chain first (eth_call). If simulation fails, nothing is sent and you see why.
  3. Summary: you see a one-line summary: network, wallet, exact amounts.
  4. Sign: your wallet asks you to approve. If you didn't sign it, it didn't happen.

The pages

  • : the launches board and token pages.
  • : trade HYPE, WHYPE, USDC and KNTQ through Uniswap V2 + V4 on Elysium.
  • : stake HYPE through Kinetiq and hold kHYPE on HyperEVM (the route Ascend's House was built on).
  • : move HYPE between HyperEVM and Elysium via the official bridge.
  • : launch on the bonding curve (opens at contract deployment).
  • : your balances and launches, read live from the chain.

Getting started

1. Connect a wallet

Click Connect wallet in the header and choose your EVM wallet, MetaMask, Rabby, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, OKX, Trust and other injected wallets are detected automatically. If your wallet is on another chain, the app asks it to switch to Elysium (chain ID 99901) or add the network for you. Your wallet connects read-first: the app can show your address and balances, but it can never move anything without a signature from you. Browsing, the board, token pages, quotes, never needs a wallet at all.

2. Mainnet by default: testnet for practice

Vector Pad runs on Elysium mainnet by default: real funds, real pools. The Mainnet / Testnet switch in the header flips the app to Elysium testnet (chain ID 99801), where HYPE has no real value, use it to rehearse the flows. Your wallet keeps the same address on both networks; balances and pools are separate per network. Every transaction is simulated before you sign, on both networks.

3. Get HYPE on Elysium

HYPE is Elysium's gas token, bridged 1:1 from HyperEVM through Kinetiq's official bridge. You need a little HYPE in your wallet beyond what you plan to spend, every transaction pays gas in HYPE. See .

4. That's it, no account

There is no sign-up, no email, no password. Your wallet address is your identity here, and disconnecting from the wallet menu in the header ends the session. Nothing about you is stored by this app.

Before your first transaction

  • Read the summary line before signing, and check the confirmation screen inside your wallet, that screen is the final word.
  • New to Elysium? Start with a small amount first while you learn the flow.
  • Make sure your wallet is actually on Elysium (chain 99901), the same address exists on every EVM chain, but balances do not move between them.

  • Price and chart: the current curve price, its change over the selected window, and a chart with timeframe switches.
  • Key stats: market cap, holders, the 1% curve fee, and HYPE liquidity in the curve.
  • Bonding curve: graduation progress toward ~85 HYPE of net buys, HYPE in the curve, tokens left, and accrued creator/protocol fees.
  • Technical details: the token contract address, status, and total supply.

Verify the contract address

Tickers are not unique, anyone can launch a token with the same name and symbol. The contract address is the only real identity. Before you trade or send anything:

  1. Copy the contract address from the token page in this app, don't retype it from a screenshot or a post.
  2. Paste it into the Elysium explorer (ElyScreener) and confirm the token name, supply and pool match what you saw here.
  3. Compare the whole address, not just the first and last few characters, impostor addresses are crafted to match at both ends.

Swap

Swap trades through Uniswap V2 and Uniswap V4 on Elysium, live now on mainnet. Every quote compares both venues and takes the better output: V2 quotes come from the verified Router02 contract (0xE783DE89a7F0408687f051e3E6D0BEb62719EbAd) via getAmountsOut; V4 quotes come from the verified V4 Quoter (0x379F556c5a748BD7Cc9baF0F7A344094Ac1814f5) against the V4 PoolManager (0xE2e105d3F7209A9DA11f83cCf3E7398a753823F1), and V4 execution runs through the Universal Router (0x1B341FDD55d56F8E08409A2C25DDFad93Eb03C1a). The route line under the quote names the venue that won.

Pairs

The swap list covers the assets with verified Elysium contracts: HYPE (native), WHYPE (0xbA876237B0F021A6c6D852d6c26E107997cC5E81), USDC (0xb47c4C8F73F009CC0C0d3f92B388d13E293F0B65, 6 decimals) and KNTQ (0xe45a355D9e9CaD2AD85F2d03e3Ee3102A036aCF0, 18 decimals). HYPE ↔ WHYPE wraps and unwraps 1:1 with no pool fee. Most V4 liquidity on Elysium pairs against native HYPE, so the working HYPE/USD market is the hook-free V4 native HYPE/USDC pool (0.30% fee), the V2 WHYPE/USDC pool is dust by comparison. WHYPE pairs route through a 1:1 unwrap/wrap plus the native V4 pool, and USDC ↔ KNTQ routes USDC → HYPE → KNTQ through V4 in two steps, each simulated before it is sent. When no venue can quote a pair, the app says “No liquidity for this pair” instead of showing a price that isn't real. Tokens launched on Vector Pad join the list after graduation, when their Uniswap V2 pool exists. On testnet, swaps are off, V2 and V4 are verified on mainnet only.

The flow

  1. Pick the pair and enter an amount. The quote updates live: expected output, price impact, minimum received and the route.
  2. If you are selling an ERC-20, the app first asks for an exact-amount approval, for V2 that is an approval for Router02; for V4 it is an approval for Permit2 plus a Permit2 allowance for the Universal Router, exact-amount and short-lived. Never an unlimited one.
  3. Every transaction in the route is simulated on-chain first. If a simulation fails, nothing further is sent.
  4. You see a one-line summary, then sign in your wallet. The final confirmation always happens there, not here.

Slippage

Slippage is the most the executed price may move against you compared with the quote. If the price moves beyond your setting before the transaction lands, the swap fails instead of filling at a bad price. The minimum-received figure in the quote already accounts for your slippage setting.

What it costs

Uniswap V2 pools charge a 0.30% fee inside the pool, and the hook-free V4 pools routed here also charge 0.30%, both kept by the pools' liquidity providers. Vector Pad adds no platform fee on swaps. Plus gas in HYPE. Full breakdown in .

Stake

Vector Pad has no staking contract of its own, staking runs in the app against Kinetiq, the liquid-staking protocol behind Elysium itself. The Stake tab signs a payable stake() call to Kinetiq's StakingManager (0x393D0B87Ed38fc779FD9611144aE649BA6082109) on HyperEVM (chain 999) and you receive kHYPE (0xfD739d4e423301CE9385c1fb8850539D657C296D), which stays in your wallet. The minimum stake and the paused flags are read live from the StakingManager (minimum was 5 HYPE at the last on-chain check), every stake is simulated before it is sent, and a summary is shown before you sign. This is the same underlying route Ascend's House was built on, Ascend's vault took kHYPE deposits, but that program has ended and takes no new deposits, so Vector Pad does not route you there. A Vector-native staking product would need its own staking contract and a real reward source; neither exists today.

Unstaking also starts in the app: approve exactly the kHYPE amount for the StakingManager, then queueWithdrawal() enters Kinetiq's queue, about 8–9 days (1-day delegation lockup + 7-day queue). After the delay, the final confirm & claim step runs on Kinetiq's official staking page, where your pending withdrawals and their IDs are shown. The Stake page reads your kHYPE balance, your HyperEVM HYPE balance and the live kHYPE→HYPE exchange rate straight from Kinetiq's contracts. Rewards accrue in the exchange rate, kHYPE does not rebase and there is no fixed APY. Swapping kHYPE on a DEX is instant at market price.

Costs

Vector Pad charges nothing for staking. Kinetiq takes 10% of staking rewards (per its docs: 70% of that fee buys KNTQ, 30% goes to its treasury), and you pay HyperEVM gas in HYPE for the transactions there.

Bridge

Elysium settles to HyperEVM, and its gas token HYPE is bridged 1:1 from HyperEVM through Kinetiq's official bridge. The same wallet address works on both chains.

Directions

  • Deposit: HyperEVM → Elysium. Credits in about 1–3 minutes. HYPE sitting on HyperCore must move to HyperEVM first (“Transfer to EVM” in the Hyperliquid app).
  • Withdraw: Elysium → HyperEVM. Start the withdrawal here; after the ~5.5-day Orbit challenge window, claim the HYPE on HyperEVM in the official bridge.

The Bridge page runs in-app against Elysium's official bridge contracts, the same contracts Kinetiq's own bridge UI uses: a deposit is one depositEth call to the official Inbox on HyperEVM (0xce59f8B65ad41C9DAE4c07A0571a39824bD92cff), and the page re-verifies that route on-chain (Inbox → Bridge → Rollup chain ID 99901) before a deposit can be sent. A withdrawal starts in-app through the ArbSys precompile on Elysium; the claim after the challenge window happens in the official Kinetiq bridge. ERC-20 tokens (kHYPE, USDC…) cross through the token gateway in that official bridge. Vector Pad adds no fee on top, you pay gas on the chain each step starts on. Never use a bridge link sent to you in a chat or DM.

Before you bridge

Check which chain your wallet is on before you sign, an address looks identical on HyperEVM and Elysium, but a balance only exists on one of them. Bridge transfers cannot be reversed. Start with a small amount the first time.

Create token

The Create wizard takes you through six steps:

  1. Identity: name, ticker, image and description.
  2. Pair: the bonding curve's parameters, paired with HYPE.
  3. Fee: the trading-fee setting you want associated with the launch.
  4. Coin type: the launch profile for the token.
  5. Payouts: wallet addresses and shares for creator payouts.
  6. Review & Execute: check the summary card, then deploy.

Bonding curve mode

One createToken call to the VectorPadLaunchpad contract (Solidity). The contract creates a fixed-supply ERC-20 (1,000,000,000 tokens, 18 decimals; no mint, burn, owner, pause or tax after creation) and holds the supply: 793.1M for sale on the curve, 206.9M reserved for the graduation pool. Virtual reserves start at 30 HYPE / 1,073,017,647 tokens; trades pay a 1% fee (0.70% protocol, 0.30% creator, accrued per token and paid out by collectFees). Your initial buy, if any, rides in the same transaction, set it to 0 to launch without buying. At ~85 HYPE of net buys the curve completes and graduates: the remaining tokens plus the collected HYPE (wrapped to WHYPE) enter the token's Uniswap V2 pool and the LP is burned to the dead address. After graduation the token page switches to Uniswap V2 quoting and curve trading switches off. Create cost: network gas only.

Deployment status: read this

The launchpad contracts are prepared and unit-tested (6/6) but NOT deployed on Elysium yet. Deployment is a separate, manual step done from the owner's wallet; when it lands, the launchpad address is published in this app and the Create, Explore and curve-trading flows switch on against it. Until then the wizard runs end-to-end but the deploy button stays off and nothing is sent.

Direct Uniswap V2 pool mode

Direct mode (mint the full supply to your wallet, then seed a Uniswap V2 pool yourself) opens after the launchpad, the token factory ships with it.

Honest note: what is metadata today

The fee percentage, coin type and payout splits picked in steps 3–5 are recorded as launch metadata. The curve contract's trade fee is fixed at 1% (0.70% protocol · 0.30% creator) and creator fees accrue to the launching wallet; holder-reward and buyback splits are not wired into the contract yet. What is fully on-chain at launch: the fixed-supply token, the curve, the fee accrual, and the burned-LP graduation pool.

Safety & risks

Verify every address

Before you send funds or sign anything, check the address in full, first characters, last characters, and the middle. Copy addresses from inside this app or your own wallet, never from screenshots, replies or direct messages. A transfer to a wrong or wrong-network address cannot be undone by anyone. Remember there are two unrelated chains named Elysium: Vector Pad runs on Kinetiq's Elysium, chain ID 99901, gas token HYPE. If your wallet shows chain 1339 or a LAVA gas token, you are on the other chain, do not sign.

$VECTOR impostor warning

$VECTOR is live on Elysium, and its only official contract address is 0x09df14bf69916d21864d5f4af75c9625303fc6cd. Any token trading as $VECTOR at a different address, on a DEX, in a group chat, anywhere, is not the official one. Verify against this address before you trade.

Your seed phrase is yours alone

Never share your seed phrase or private key, with anyone, anywhere. Vector Pad never asks for them: every signature happens inside your own wallet. Any site, form, support agent or "verification" step that asks for your seed phrase is a scam, full stop.

Confirmed means final

Once a transaction is confirmed on-chain it cannot be reversed, edited or refunded, not by Vector Pad, not by the protocol, not by anyone. That is why every flow here simulates first and shows you a summary before your wallet asks you to sign. Read both.

Smart-contract risk

Vector Pad routes through third-party contracts, Uniswap V2 and V4, the bridge, and (once deployed) the Vector Pad launchpad. Bugs, exploits, admin actions or outages in those contracts can cause loss, and new pools and tokens carry more risk than established ones. Bridged assets additionally carry the risk of the bridge itself.

Memecoin risk

Most newly launched tokens lose most or all of their value, quickly and permanently. Never spend funds you cannot afford to lose entirely, and assume any token, including one you create, may become illiquid or worthless. Nothing in this app or these docs is financial advice.

FAQ

Do I need an account to use Vector Pad?

No. There is no sign-up and no account, connect an EVM wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, OKX…) when you want to transact, and browse freely without one.

Does Vector Pad hold my funds?

Never. Vector Pad is a non-custodial front-end: your assets stay in your wallet or in the protocol you are using (a Uniswap V2 pool, the curve contract, the bridge), and every transaction is signed by you.

Which Elysium is this: there are two?

Kinetiq's Elysium: an Arbitrum Orbit layer-2 that settles to HyperEVM, chain ID 99901, gas token HYPE. It is unrelated to the older Vulcan Forged gaming chain that also uses the Elysium name (chain 1339, LAVA). If your wallet shows 1339, switch networks before signing anything.

Is $VECTOR live? What is its contract address?

Yes, $VECTOR is live on Elysium. Its official contract address is 0x09df14bf69916d21864d5f4af75c9625303fc6cd. Anything trading under the same name at a different address is an impostor, verify against this address before you trade.

What does it cost to launch a coin?

Bonding curve: network gas only, no seed, no pool fee; the graduation pool is paid from the curve's collected HYPE. Launches open when the launchpad contract is deployed on Elysium. See for the full table.

Why did my transaction stop before signing?

Simulation failed, so nothing was broadcast, that is the safety pattern working. The usual causes are an amount above your balance, a missing token approval, or a pool without enough liquidity. Check the amount and the pair first.

Can a transaction be undone or refunded?

No. Once a transaction is confirmed on-chain it is final. Always check the summary line and your wallet's confirmation screen before signing.

Does Vector Pad take a cut of swaps or launches?

No platform fee anywhere. Swap pools charge the standard 0.30% pool fee (Uniswap V2 pools and the hook-free Uniswap V4 pools routed here), and the curve charges its fixed 1% (0.70% protocol · 0.30% creator) once deployed, Vector Pad adds nothing on top of either.
